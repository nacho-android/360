# Beck’s Micro-Adventure

A mobile-friendly static HTML/JavaScript/CSS birthday game.

## Story

Beck, microeconomist at the University of Sydney, accidentally shrinks herself into one of her own microeconomic experiments. She must solve the lab protocol, supply-and-demand equilibrium, elasticity, and a birthday-table gauntlet to find the golden key and get home for wine, chocolate licorice bullets, and cake.

## Play

- Keyboard: `WASD` or arrow keys
- Interact: `E`, `Space`, or the `✦` touch button
- Mobile: on-screen D-pad and interact button
- Sound starts after the Start button because mobile browsers require a user gesture for audio.
