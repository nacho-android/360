# Character and Cast Guide

## Core family and pets

### Alan
- **Role:** Playable protagonist, research veterinarian, problem-solver
- **Personality:** Kind, pragmatic, witty under pressure
- **Visual style:** Teal clinical jacket, satchel, quick stride, slightly heroic exhaustion
- **Dialogue flavor:** Warm deadpan, calm competence, occasional “right, okay, new problem” energy
- **Gameplay function:** Exploration, quests, gadget use, minigames, movement mastery
- **Relationship to Alan:** Alan is Alan
- **Comic potential:** Treats absurdity as a scheduling issue
- **Offers:** Everything; he is the player character

### Mariko
- **Role:** Veterinary cardiologist, major narrative anchor
- **Personality:** Capable, playful, sharp, caring
- **Visual style:** Rose-gold accents, clinic coat, composed presence
- **Dialogue flavor:** Dry banter, warm clarity, elite eyebrow work
- **Gameplay function:** Clinic missions, emotional grounding, upgrade route
- **Relationship to Alan:** Wife and trusted co-conspirator in surviving chaos
- **Comic potential:** Instantly sees through nonsense
- **Offers:** Quests, lore, emotional support, late unlock path
