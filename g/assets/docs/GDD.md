# Game Design Brief — Alan: Campus of Controlled Chaos

## High concept

A funny, heartfelt, mobile-first, replayable isometric campus adventure where Alan, a research veterinarian, explores a whimsical science campus, solves linked crises, helps colleagues, manages energy and priorities, unlocks gadgets, and balances heroism with home life.

## Core loop

1. Explore dense zones
2. Talk to NPCs and accept objectives
3. Solve tasks and minigames
4. Collect resources / lore / collectibles
5. Unlock upgrades and improve efficiency
6. Progress chapters and persistent world state
7. Return to earlier spaces with new tools and contextual dialogue

## Release-one feature pillars

- Short-session friendly while still story-forward
- Touch-first and static-hosting safe
- Warm, character-based comedy
- Humane animal portrayal focused on care, logistics, and teamwork
- Strong replay value via achievements, collectibles, buffs, and hard mode

## Chapters

### Chapter 1 — Morning Vector
Recover the badge, stabilize coffee, calm the sheep committee, repair imaging.

### Chapter 2 — Procedure Pirates
Investigate reflective tags, follow Batty’s clue, complete the rooftop drone mission, unwind with Noah.

### Chapter 3 — Bluey Protocol
Visit Mariko’s clinic, help Ross calibrate a pulse rig, follow Bluey into the dream reef.

### Finale — Controlled Chaos
Restore backup power, control a runaway coffee cart sequence, negotiate with sheep, seal the imaging loop.

## Tone

- Smart and warm, never mean
- Character humor over cheap parody
- Science-adjacent and accessible
- Slightly chaotic, but emotionally grounded

## Difficulty philosophy

- Gentle onboarding
- Clear objective guidance
- Early forgiveness
- Optional hard mode unlocked after ending

## Mobile stance

Landscape is recommended because it improves world readability and thumb spacing, but portrait remains playable.
